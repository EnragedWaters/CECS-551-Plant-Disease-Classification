What the visuals mean, selection policy, and attribution

Feature maps show numerical responses inside the network. Colors are not attention, a disease mask, or proof of why a prediction happened. Channels 0–3 are fixed; a pale tile may simply be quiet for this photo. Within each stage, both models share the same scale: blue is negative, pale is zero, orange is positive. Scales differ between stages.

The 576-value summary grid is a display arrangement, not image coordinates. Category bars are final softmax scores, not intermediate guesses or calibrated probabilities. Dataset labels are annotations, not independently verified diagnoses.
